| Chat Rules

We want everyone to enjoy themselves and feel welcome here. Please abide by these rules.

Article 1: Civil Behaviour

  • 1.1 Avoid bombarding the chat with too many emojis, repeated messages, or level-up attempts.
  • 1.2 Don't post links anywhere on the server or promote yourself or others.
  • 1.3 Greet and engage new members. Avoid being impolite, eerie, or violent. Use correct display names (no forced nicknames).
  • 1.4 Show respect for staff. No offensive names/PFPs (staff may alter them at any time).
  • 1.5 Never share someone else's pictures or personal info without consent.
Edge Case: "I was just joking around!"

Intent does not matter if the behavior disrupts the chat or makes others uncomfortable. Staff discretion applies.

Article 2: Following Instructions

  • 2.1 You must change the topic if a staff member requests it. Noncompliance leads to kick/ban.
  • 2.2 Avoid tagging staff members unless it's for a significant occasion.
  • 2.3 Avoid arguing with moderators or staff. Their choices are final.

Article 3: Conflict Resolution

  • 3.1 Blocking and moving on is the best course of action when someone is bothering you.
  • 3.2 It is assumed that you possess the common sense to recognise sarcasm and puns.

| Community Rules

⚠️ The spirit of the rule is more important than its exact wording. Moderators have the last word.

01

Follow Discord Terms of Service

Abide by Discord guidelines. Violation can result in severe action, including permanent removal.

02

Keep Content Appropriate for All Ages

No NSFW content (extreme violence, self-harm, explicit sexual content). Discussion related to such content must not share explicit media.

Edge Case: "It was just a meme/art!"

Drawn art, anime, or "joke" images that depict sexually explicit acts or extreme gore are still heavily strictly prohibited.

03

Good Attitude & Respect Staff

Be respectful. Disruptive/aggressive behavior is not tolerated. Address mod actions in tickets, NOT in public channels. Evading punishments leads to bans.

04

Speak Hinglish & Follow Channel Rules

Official language is Hinglish (scriptures in English) so everyone understands. Read channel pins as some channels have specific exemptions/rules.

05

No Spam or Advertising

No disruptive self-promotion, duplicate messages, wall-texts, or excessive pings/emojis. Selling server growth methods or posting scams results in an instant ban.

06

Respect Others' Privacy

Do not share real names, addresses, or other social media accounts without permission. Doxxing leads to immediate removal.

07

Profile Criteria

No unreadable/disruptive names. No impersonation of others. We will moderate default user profiles if they violate server rules.

08

No Hate Speech or Sensitive Topics

Discrimination, hate speech, or debates on politics, religion, violence, or regulated goods are forbidden. Report suspicious DMs to staff immediately.

09

Staff Intervention & Media Spam

If staff are pinged for suspicious VC activity, they may join to investigate. Spamming videos, GIFs, or images is prohibited.

To contest a warning, ping @Staff Team or open a ticket and INCLUDE YOUR CASE ID.
