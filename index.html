01. Chat Rules

We want everyone to enjoy themselves and feel welcome here. Please abide by these rules. Tap a card to read the full details.

Article 1: Civil Behaviour

No spamming, no inappropriate names, and respect privacy.

  • 1.1

    Avoid bombarding the chat with too many emojis, repeated messages, or level-up attempts.

  • 1.2

    Don't post links anywhere on the server or promote yourself or others.

  • 1.3

    Greet and engage new members in discussions. Avoid being impolite, eerie, or violent. Don't give members nicknames; use their correct display name.

  • 1.4

    Pay attention to and show respect for staff. Refrain from offensive names/PFPs; staff may alter them.

  • 1.5

    Never share someone else's pictures or personal info without consent.

Article 2: Following Instructions

Listen to staff, avoid arguing, and don't abuse pings.

  • 2.1

    You must change the topic if a staff member requests it. A kick/ban may be imposed for noncompliance.

  • 2.2

    Avoid tagging staff members unless it's for a significant occasion.

  • 2.3

    Avoid arguing with moderators or staff. Their choices are final.

Article 3: Conflict Resolution

Use the block button and possess common sense.

  • 3.1

    Blocking and moving on is the best course of action when someone is bothering you.

  • 3.2

    It is assumed that you possess the common sense to recognise sarcasm and puns.

02. Community Rules

The spirit of the rule is more important than its exact wording. Moderators have the last word on all warnings and rule interpretations. Willfully pushing the bounds of a rule will receive a warning.

1

Follow Discord Terms of Service

Abide by standard Discord guidelines.

Please abide by the rules and policies set out in Discord Guidelines and Terms. Depending on the violation, action taken can be as severe as removal from the server.
2

Keep Content Appropriate for All Ages

No NSFW, extreme violence, or self-harm content.

Posting of NSFW content - defined as extreme violence, self-harm, and explicit sexual content - is strictly not allowed. Discussion related to such content should refrain from sharing explicit media. Violations are subject to staff judgment.
3

Have a Good Attitude & Be Respectful

Address mod actions in tickets, not public chat.

All members will be respectful and kind. Any disruptive, aggressive, or disrespectful behavior will not be tolerated. Follow directions from staff and address mod actions in tickets, not public forums. Circumventing punishments results in removal.
4

Speak Hinglish & Follow Channel Rules

Keep conversations understandable for everyone.

The official language is Hinglish and scriptures followed are English. Please converse in this language so everyone understands. Read channel descriptions and pins for specific rules or exemptions.
5

Avoid Excessive Advertising and Spam

No disruptive self-promo or spreading scams.

Self-promotion, duplicate messages, excessive media, mentions, selling growth methods, and harmful links are prohibited. Breaking this or the Ethical Community Growth Guidelines is grounds for immediate removal.
6

Respect Others' Privacy

Never share private info without permission.

Don't share anyone else's private information (real names, addresses, social media) without their permission. Malintentions will lead to removal.
7

User Profile Criteria

No disruptive names or impersonation.

Do not use unreadable names. Profiles must adhere to rules and cannot impersonate others. Staff reserve the right to take action based on profile severity.
8

No Hate Speech or Sensitive Material

Discrimination and sensitive topics are forbidden.

Discrimination, hate speech, or discussions on politics, religion, violence, or regulated goods are strictly forbidden. Report suspicious DMs to staff.
9

Staff Intervention

Staff may review chat and join VCs to investigate.

If a user pings staff regarding suspicious activity, they may review the chat first. If inappropriate activity is found, they may join the VC to investigate and take action.
10

Media Spam

Spamming videos, GIFs, or images is not allowed.

Spamming videos, images, GIFs, or any other media content is not allowed and may result in moderation actions. Use appropriate channels and include case IDs when contesting warnings.
