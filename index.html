01. Chat Rules

We want everyone to enjoy themselves and feel welcome here. Please abide by these rules. Tap a card to expand.

Article 1: Civil Behaviour

No spamming, no inappropriate names, and respect privacy.

  • 1.1

    Avoid bombarding the chat with too many emojis, repeated messages, or level-up attempts.

  • 1.2

    Don't post links anywhere on the server or promote yourself or others.

  • 1.3

    Greet and engage new members in discussions. Avoid being impolite, eerie, or violent. Don't give members nicknames; instead, use their correct display name.

  • 1.4

    Pay attention to and show respect for the staff members. Please refrain from using offensive names or profile pictures, as staff members have the right to alter them at any time.

  • 1.5

    Never share someone else's pictures or personal information without that person's consent. Also, share your own personal information at your own risk.

Article 2: Following Instructions

Listen to staff, avoid arguing, and don't abuse pings.

  • 2.1

    You must change the topic if a staff member requests it. A kick or ban may be imposed for noncompliance.

  • 2.2

    Avoid tagging staff members unless it's for a significant occasion.

  • 2.3

    Avoid arguing with moderators or staff. Their choices are final.

Article 3: Conflict Resolution

Use the block button and possess common sense.

  • 3.1

    Blocking and moving on is the best course of action when someone is bothering you.

  • 3.2

    It is assumed that you possess the common sense to recognise sarcasm and puns.

02. Community Rules

The spirit of the rule is more important than its exact wording. Moderators have the last word. Pushing boundaries will result in warnings for toxic attitudes.

1

Follow Discord Terms of Service

Abide by Discord guidelines. Violations lead to severe action.

Please abide by the rules and policies set out in Discord Guidelines and Terms of Service. Depending on the violation of these terms, action taken can be as severe as removal from the server.

2

Keep Content Appropriate

No NSFW, extreme violence, or self-harm content.

Posting of NSFW content - defined as extreme violence, self-harm, and explicit sexual content - is strictly not allowed. Discussion related to such content should refrain from sharing explicit media. Violations are subject to staff judgment.

3

Respect Server Staff & Members

Address mod actions in tickets, not in public chat.

On this server, all members will be respectful and kind to each other. Any disruptive, aggressive, or disrespectful behavior, or any form of nuisance, will not be tolerated. Members are to follow directions from server staff and address any questions regarding mod actions in the appropriate channels (Tickets) and not in a public forum. Circumventing punishments will result in removal.

4

Speak Hinglish

Official language is Hinglish (scriptures in English).

The official language of the server is Hinglish and scriptures followed are English. Please have conversations with other users of the server in this language because this allows everyone to understand each other best. Read channel pins for channel-specific exemptions.

5

No Spam or Advertising

No disruptive self-promo or spreading scams.

It is prohibited to self-promote disruptively, spam the channel with duplicate messages, or to use images, links, emojis, or mentions in an excessive manner. Selling methods for server growth is strictly not allowed, nor is the spreading of harmful links or scams.

6

Respect Privacy

Do not share personal info without permission.

Don't share anyone else's private information, like real names, addresses, other social media accounts, etc., without their permission. Anyone doing so with malintentions is liable to be removed from the server.

7

No Hate Speech or Impersonation

Discrimination, sensitive topics, and fake profiles are forbidden.

All aspects of your profile must not attempt to impersonate other persons. Discrimination, hate speech, or discussions on sensitive topics like politics, religion, violence, or regulated goods are strictly forbidden.

Beware of unexpected DMs on Discord. If you receive suspicious links, report them to the mod team via tickets.

8

Staff Intervention & Media Spam

Staff can investigate VCs; Do not spam media.

If a user pings the staff regarding suspicious activity, staff members may join the VC to investigate further and take necessary action. Furthermore, spamming videos, images, GIFs, or any other media content is not allowed.
