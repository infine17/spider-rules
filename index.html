01. Chat Rules

Article 1: Civil Behaviour

  • 1.1 Avoid bombarding the chat with too many emojis, repeated messages, or level-up attempts.
  • 1.2 Don't post links anywhere on the server or promote yourself or others.
  • 1.3 Greet and engage new members. Avoid being impolite, eerie, or violent. Use correct display names.
  • 1.4 Show respect for staff. No offensive names/PFPs (staff may alter them at any time).
  • 1.5 Never share someone else's pictures or personal info without consent.
Edge Case: "I was just joking around!" ▼

Intent does not matter if the behavior disrupts the chat or makes others uncomfortable. Staff discretion applies 100% of the time.

Article 2: Following Instructions

  • 2.1 You must change the topic if a staff member requests it. Noncompliance leads to kick/ban.
  • 2.2 Avoid tagging staff members unless it's for a significant occasion.
  • 2.3 Avoid arguing with moderators or staff. Their choices are final.

Article 3: Conflict Resolution

  • 3.1 Blocking and moving on is the best course of action when someone is bothering you.
  • 3.2 It is assumed that you possess the common sense to recognise sarcasm and puns. Don't weaponize reports over jokes.

02. Community Rules

⚠️

The spirit of the rule is more important than its exact wording. Moderators have the last word. Pushing the boundaries of rules on purpose will result in action.

01

Follow Discord Terms of Service

Abide by Discord guidelines. Violation can result in severe action, including permanent removal.

02

Appropriate Content

No NSFW content (extreme violence, self-harm, explicit sexual content). Discussion related to such content must not share explicit media.

Edge Case: "It was just a meme/anime!" ▼

Drawn art, anime, or "joke" images that depict sexually explicit acts or extreme gore are heavily strictly prohibited. Context does not save you here.

03

Respect Server Staff

Disruptive/aggressive behavior is not tolerated. Address mod actions in tickets, NOT in public channels. Evading punishments leads to instant bans.

04

Speak Hinglish

Official language is Hinglish (scriptures in English) so everyone understands. Read channel pins as some channels have specific exemptions/rules.

05

No Spam or Advertising

No disruptive self-promotion, duplicate messages, or wall-texts. Selling server growth methods or posting scams results in an instant ban.

08

No Hate Speech or Sensitive Topics

Discrimination, hate speech, or debates on politics, religion, violence, or regulated goods are forbidden. Report suspicious DMs to staff immediately.
